**Author:** Sina Montazeri

**Date:** November 25, 2026

[Paper](prime-count-laplace-transforms-and-the-geometry-of-zeta-zeros.pdf)

## Version

First standalone edition. The paper relates the logarithmic prime-count transform to the geometry of individual zeta-zero modes and compares it with the Laplace transform in the original prime-count variable. This material was developed in the [first November 25 edition of *A Geometric View of Einstein–Yang–Mills Energy and Stress*](../A-Geometric-View-of-Einstein-Yang-Mills-Energy-and-Stress-November-25-2026/).
