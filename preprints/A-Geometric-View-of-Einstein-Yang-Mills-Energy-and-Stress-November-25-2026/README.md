## Version

Third dated edition, following the [November 18 edition](../A-Geometric-View-of-Einstein-Yang-Mills-Energy-and-Stress-November-18-2026/). This edition develops the phase-plane construction through coupled field responses, closed geometry, energy and stress, collective mass, and the excitation spectrum. It includes a companion containing Appendices A–G, expanded geometric figures, the finite periodic Wilson-theory gap result at fixed lattice spacing and sufficiently strong bare coupling, and the arithmetic fixed-radius criterion equivalent to the Riemann hypothesis.

The arithmetic criterion states an equivalence with the Riemann hypothesis. The positive gap result retains its stated lattice-spacing and coupling conditions.
