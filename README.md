<h2 align="center"><strong>Physics for Humanity</strong></h2>

This research program started as an investigation of dynamics and its structures from first principles. It began with phenomenology and numerology and, hopefully, made it past the Dunning–Kruger peaks encountered along the way. Its goal has always been, and will always remain, to help in the continuing development of a coherent physical theory that benefits humanity.

### To Graduate Students

This research aims to make full use of, and give credit to, the research community. The results in these manuscripts were first introduced in [the original theoretical-physics repository](https://github.com/czerongit/theoratical-physics). Feel free to check if your professor or research group has a theorem, lemma or proposition that could:

1. replace any part of the manuscripts with a citation to your work.
2. be cited for context, contrast, counterarguments, etc.

This could be from their books, papers, preprints or other material published before November 2025.

If you find any instances, please send me an email describing exactly what you would like replaced and/or cited and why.

### Looking for Collaborators

If you are a researcher in physics or mathematics and are interested in coauthoring a formal publication based on these preprints, here is the easiest process I could come up with.

1. Please contact me at `sm[at]nullmetric[dot]com` to discuss the collaboration. If you are a leading researcher, your name or website is enough for the initial email.
2. Please open a pull request to this repository with your proposed changes, contributions, names, and affiliations.
3. Either you or I can submit the manuscript to an official venue.

### Versions and Citations

Papers are organized in dated directories under `preprints/`. Each edition supplies its own BibTeX citation and version note. Corrections and revisions receive new dated editions, preserving earlier editions for citation.

To cite a manuscript, use the BibTeX block in its release directory.

Repository organization follows the [OpenAI mathematics collection](https://github.com/openai/math).
